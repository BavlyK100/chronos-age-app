# CHRONOS: Life Metrics & Age Engine (Flask Web App)

A professional, responsive, and animated web application built with Python and Flask to calculate exact age, life breakdown statistics, astronomical space voyage, biological milestones, and live life tickers.

---

## Highlights & Features

- **Professional Aesthetic**:
  - Deep obsidian & indigo glassmorphism UI.
  - High-performance responsive layouts for Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop.
  - Fluid CSS transitions, glowing accent blobs, and subtle hover animations.
- **Header & Footer**:
  - Sticky glass header with live system clock and social profile icons (**GitHub** and **LinkedIn**).
  - Modern footer with copyright, links, and quick branding.
- **Precision Age Calculation**:
  - Exact breakdown into **Years, Months, and Days**.
  - Total Days, Total Weeks, Total Full Months, Total Solar Orbits, Total Hours, and Total Minutes.
- **Delightful Surprises Included**:
  1. **Cosmic Space Voyage**: Calculates how many millions of kilometers you've traveled through space on Earth's orbit around the Sun since the day you were born!
  2. **Biological Milestones**: Estimates of total heartbeats pumped, breaths taken, and days spent in dreamland (sleep).
  3. **Astrology & Identity**: The exact weekday you were born, Western Zodiac sign + personality traits, and Chinese Zodiac animal.
  4. **Live Ticking Life Clock**: A real-time ticker showing your total lived seconds ticking forward every second!
  5. **Birthday Celebration & Confetti**: Automatic confetti shower using `canvas-confetti` when the entered date is today's birthday!
